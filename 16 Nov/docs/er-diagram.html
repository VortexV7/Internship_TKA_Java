Users & roles

One role has many users. Users post jobs, are assigned as recruiters on applications, record status changes, submit interview feedback and generate activity logs.

Candidates & skills

A candidate has many candidate_skills; a skill appears in many of them. One skill per candidate is flagged is_primary.

Companies & jobs

A company posts many jobs. A job requires many job_skills; a skill is required by many jobs. mandatory_flag marks must-have skills.

The pipeline

Candidate → application → interview round → feedback → offer → placement. Each (candidate, job) pair applies once; each interview gets one feedback; each application gets at most one offer, and each offer at most one placement.

History & audit

Every status change writes one row to application_history (append-only). activity_logs records who did what, in which module.

Lookups

qualifications and locations are standalone master tables for dropdown values, so the same qualification or city is never typed two different ways.
